# Deferred platform subsystems

## Admin portal

- Role-based access for super admin, admin, moderator, content editor, finance and support.
- Manage users, active plans, service access, experts, bookings, programmes, scholarships, jobs and reference data.
- Review queues for programme/job data, expert credentials, reported content and document-verification requests.
- Audit history for sensitive actions, permission changes and content publishing.
- Operational dashboards for subscriptions, usage, consultations, applications and support issues.

## Join as Expert and expert portal

- Public expert application with identity, credentials, specializations, destinations, languages and service proposals.
- Admin/moderator verification and approval workflow.
- Expert profile, pricing, availability, consultation requests and calendar management.
- Permission-based access to user-shared case summaries and document metadata.
- Consultation notes, recommendations, follow-up actions, cancellation and rescheduling.
- Payout, invoice and performance reporting requirements for a production implementation.

These subsystems are intentionally deferred until the user-side study, career and job journeys are validated.
